# Expedition to the Barrier Peaks
## Chapter 5 — The Crash Site Environs
### D&D 2024 (5.5e) Conversion
### *Optional Wilderness Chapter*

**Overview:** This chapter fleshes out the area immediately surrounding the crashed spaceship. It provides hooks for ongoing campaigns, identifies the source of the frontier raids, and populates the Barrier Peaks with meaningful encounters. It is entirely **optional** — the original adventure begins at Area 1-12 (the ship entrance) with no wilderness context. Include it only if the party needs more investigation, wilderness roleplay, or if the "mysterious cave" reveal benefits from a full buildup.

**Design Note:** Including this chapter risks tipping the party's hand about the adventure's identity early — describing the attackers' alien appearance and the floating metal "altar" before they enter the ship reduces the impact of the reveal. The DM should weigh this against the benefits of a fuller wilderness arc.

---

## GENERAL FEATURES

**Terrain.** The Barrier Peaks are a chain of rugged, steep mountains with narrow valleys and dells carved by streams. Small woods of pine, aspen, and deciduous trees cling to the lower river valleys. Thunderstorms are common in summer; flash floods are a constant danger. The peaks visible from the region are genuinely impassable — all travel is limited to the valleys and dells.

**Travel.** Direct overland travel is impossible in this region. All movement is through valleys and dells — the entire Barrier Peaks exterior counts as **difficult terrain**. Few trails exist beyond animal paths. The party should expect 4–6 hours of travel per hex in this environment.

**Scale.** The encounter areas are distributed across roughly a 6-hex radius around the ship's crash site (Area 1-12). The DM should adapt distances to the party's travel pace and the campaign's needs. Refer to Map 1 for hex positions.

## RANDOM ENCOUNTER DESCRIPTIONS

---

### Siren
A loud blaring noise sounds five times from the direction of the spaceship, then stops. This is the alert siren triggered whenever the Level VI cargo bay door opens (see Area 16, Level VI). A creature within **five hexes** of the ship automatically identifies the direction. Further creatures must succeed on a **DC = 5 + 1 per additional hex** Wisdom (Perception) check to locate the source.

---

### Android
One android (see Level I Appendix B) in alien shimmering clothing patrols this area. It attacks if noticed but will not pursue beyond its patrol zone. If the party has not yet encountered androids, emphasize the alien beauty of its appearance — then the wires and circuit boards when it's destroyed.

---

### Adult Blue Dragon
Ninyoldah (see Area 1-4C) is overhead, either hunting or returning to her lair. She takes note of the party's position and may choose to land and intimidate, demand tribute, or simply ignore them if they are insignificant-looking. She will not attack unprovoked unless the party acts hostile.

---

### Curious Metal Fragment
A shield-sized piece of jagged metal is discovered mostly buried in rocky soil. Strange characters are etched into it, indecipherable to the party. A *comprehend languages* spell reveals: **"Trans-Plutonian Spaceyard #5791."** The meaning is entirely beyond any character's understanding. More fragments found later are similar in size but unlabeled — all pieces that broke from the ship on impact, only recently exposed by the earthquakes.

---

### Rockslide
Loose stones and soil give way as the party passes below a high slope. Every creature must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **11 (2d10) bludgeoning damage** and being knocked prone on a failure, or half damage and staying upright on a success. Mounted creatures whose mounts fail the save make their own save at **disadvantage**.

---

### Cave Bears
A mother cave bear (use **polar bear** statistics) forages with its cub (use **black bear** statistics) close by. The bears avoid the party unless any creature attempts to interact with or harm the cub — in that case, the mother attacks fearlessly and without retreat.

---

### Ettin
A **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check** hears loud voices arguing in Giant, approaching from a random direction. The two heads debate: head north to hunt mountain goats, or south to fish eels in the river. If the ettin sees the party, both heads immediately agree that the party is the better meal.

---

### Giant Goats
The party blunders into a herd of **2d8 giant goats**. Half the adults are aggressive males who charge immediately. The females and young flee to higher ground. The males fight for **1d6+2 rounds** or until driven off, then rejoin their herd.

---

### Hill Giants
**1d6 hill giants** from the lower foothills are ranging up into the mountains looking for food and loot. They attack the party. They have no knowledge of the pacifier robot or its troops — presenting them as a potential red herring is useful if the party suspects giants are behind the raids.

---

### Purple Worm
The tramping of the horde's feet and recent earthquakes have drawn a purple worm near the surface. The ground trembles for **2 rounds** before it bursts from below to attack. If the entire party is airborne, the worm doesn't detect them and retreats after the trembling subsides.

---

### Bulette
A bulette has been disturbed by the increased traffic and activity. Roll initiative from below the surface. On a **1–3:** it attacks a random party member immediately. On a **4–6:** it bursts up near an animal or domesticated creature traveling with the party (mounts, pack animals) and attacks the animal first. If an animal is injured and bleeding nearby, and a party member succeeds on a **DC 12 Charisma (Animal Handling or Persuasion) check** to invoke sympathy toward the creature, the bulette takes the prey instead and retreats.

---

### Stone Giant (Frummach)
This stone giant is a member of the clan in Area 1-7, returning from a "dream walk" (see Area 1-7). He is unaware of his patriarch's death or the pacifier robot. He avoids an obviously strong party but fights if attacked, preferring thrown boulders.

If the party treats him with respect and demonstrates they mean no harm (DC 12 Charisma [Persuasion] check), Frummach becomes cautiously friendly. He can lead the party to his clan's cavern (Area 1-7) and will introduce them warmly if they accompany him — granting the party a peaceful reception from the grieving giants rather than a hostile one.

---

### Type One/Two Biological Entities
**Type one** (2d4 individuals): A squad that has gotten separated from the main body or is riddled with cloning defects. They attack anything they encounter.

**Type two** (1d4 individuals): On a "softening" patrol, moving ahead of the horde to deal with potential threats. They attack immediately and do not flee.

Both types use the stat blocks from Level VII Appendix B.

### AREA 1-1 — THE BARON'S KEEP

*A motte-and-bailey castle sits atop a craggy hill. Three sides of the hill are steep and jagged; a narrow trail winds up the southern side to a stone gatehouse. The bailey wall is a mixture of stone and sharpened timbers. A green-and-white pennant flaps in the mountain wind. Soldiers in steel caps and leather cuirasses are visible at the gatehouse.*

This is the keep of **Baron Urhar**, Warden of the Northern Watches — the last warden standing in the wake of the recent attacks and the quest-giver for this chapter. The keep houses **200 men-at-arms** along with the Baron and his wife, the Baroness Elesha. The atmosphere is tense and fearful; the Baron expects the next attack at any time.

**Baron Urhar** is a former soldier in his late thirties, balding with a dark fringe, tall and broad. He speaks plainly — especially to fellow warriors — and earned his title through battlefield victories. He welcomes the party warmly, offers quarters and a feast, and briefs them on what little is known.

**Baroness Elesha** is short, hearty, with curly dark hair bound in braids and sharp insight into others' motivations. She is more formal than the Baron and genuinely looks after his political interests. Their marriage was political but she is fond of him.

**Development.** So long as the party doesn't make impossible demands or overstay their welcome, the Baron provides:
- Quarters and stabling whenever present at the keep
- Directions to the nearest attacked castle (Area 1-3) with a scout guide
- A **cadre of 20 men-at-arms** to guard the party's camp and mounts if the party succeeds on a **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check** — these guards will not enter the cave but will wait up to four days before returning to the keep

If the DM prefers the party to search independently, the Baron knows only that the attackers "come from the farthest side of the Tumbledown River." If the DM wants to accelerate, his scouts have already located the cave entrance (Area 1-12) and the invaders' encampment (Area 1-6).

---

### WHAT THE BARON KNOWS

The Baron did not observe the attackers directly, but rode to a neighbor's aid and arrived too late. His observations:

- **Few tracks** at the sacked stronghold, but some were bare, bestial feet unlike any known animal — and some from boots with ridged rubber soles (android treads)
- **Many bodies bore burn marks** as if stabbed with a hot poker; others were set ablaze entirely; others showed massive blunt-force and falling injuries (pacifier robot's tractor/repulsion beams)
- The attack appeared **not to be driven by greed** — coins were melted in place, bodies not stripped. The goal was total destruction
- A **large blue dragon** has been sighted frequently on the western side of the Tumbledown River, but it has left the Baron's forces alone; the damage at the sacked keep does not look like dragon work

### AREA 1-2 — THE TUMBLEDOWN RIVER

*A fast-moving creek rushes through a stone-choked valley, frothing and misting through rapids. The river is narrow enough that a strong arm could throw a spear bank to bank, but the current looks fast and the spray is cold.*

The Tumbledown marks the northern border of the kingdom. **No bridge or ford exists**. The party must cross to reach the wilderness where the ship lies.

**Crossing the River (20 feet wide, 3 feet deep):**
- *Leap from rock to rock:* **DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check**. Success = across safely.
- *Wade through:* **DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check** to avoid being swept off feet.
- *Scouting a better crossing:* **DC 15 Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) check**, 1d4 hours of searching — reduces all crossing DCs by 10.

**Failed crossing:** **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** — failure: **10 (3d6) bludgeoning + 4 (1d8) cold damage**, carried 30 feet downstream. The creature can attempt **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** at the start of each turn to grab a boulder or swim to shore; until it succeeds, it repeats the Dexterity save each turn.

**Discovery at the riverbank.** While scouting for a crossing point, the party finds a waterlogged corpse half-submerged along the rocky shore: a 6-foot-tall muscular humanoid with animalistic features — bat-like ears, a short muzzle, canine rear legs with clawed feet. Dressed only in torn dirty pants. Many broken bones; crushed skull. **DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check:** Creature has been dead 3–7 days; cause of death was drowning, but other injuries came from being battered against rocks in the rapids. Its species is entirely unknown to any character. (This is a type one biological entity that fell into the river during a patrol and was swept downstream.)

### AREA 1-3 — THE SACKED STRONGHOLD

*The smashed remains of a stone-and-timber watchtower fortress occupy a rocky prominence overlooking a valley. The outer walls have been breached and knocked inward by tremendous force. Blackened stone and burned timbers are everywhere. Bodies in various states of decay lie where they fell.*

This is the closest stronghold to the Barrier Peaks that was destroyed by the pacifier robot's forces. The Baron's scout can lead the party here directly.

**Evidence of the Attack:**
- **Burn patterns** on bodies and stonework: precise, as if from a focused energy source — not the scattered burns of a dragon's breath or torches, but narrow surgical scorching followed by wholesale incineration
- **Structural damage** that defies explanation: sections of stone wall punched inward as if struck by massive blunt force at a high point on the wall — consistent with a tractor/repulsion beam slamming the wall from above
- **No looting:** Coins, weapons, and valuables remain where they fell; the attack was not motivated by plunder
- **Footprints (DC 12 Wisdom [Survival]):** Two types — bare, clawed canine feet (biological entities) and ridged-sole boots (androids). The tracks move in organized patterns, not the random paths of a raiding mob
- **A single surviving witness** (DC 15 Wisdom [Perception] to notice in the rubble): A young stablehand who hid in the manure pile during the attack. He is in shock but can describe the following:
  - *"They came from the sky — or not exactly, they came over the ridge. There were dog-people but also... beautiful people? With bright metal clothes. And then this thing — like an altar — floating. The altar started shooting fire and everything burned."*
  - He can provide rough direction of travel to the southeast (toward Area 1-6 encampment)

### AREA 1-4 — NINYOLDAH'S CAVE COMPLEX (ENTRANCE)

*A cave mouth opens in the mountainside, partially hidden behind a rockfall. The entrance tunnel is low — perhaps 10 feet high, 15 feet wide — and descends into darkness. The air coming from within is oddly warm and carries a strange metallic scent. Purple-red streaks of mineral run through the cave walls.*

The caves wind through the mountain for several hundred feet before reaching the dragon's main complex. The purple-red deposits throughout are **lightning stone** (see sidebar).

**Dragon Scent.** A **DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana or Nature) check** identifies the metallic smell as dragon. On a failure, it simply smells wrong — volcanic, almost, but not quite.

**Cloakers.** The initial entry passage is home to **3 cloakers** (*Monster Manual*) that cling to the ceiling, appearing as rock formations or shadows in the darkness. They serve as Ninyoldah's early-warning system; when they use their **Moan** action, the sound echoes down to the dragon's den and alerts her to intruders.

If the cloakers are bypassed quietly (DC 15 Dexterity [Stealth] group check), Ninyoldah has no warning and the party approaches Area 1-4B without triggering her breath weapon ambush. If the cloakers moan — combat noise, light sources, or failed stealth — Ninyoldah prepares her breath weapon at the chokepoint in Area 1-4B.

---

**LIGHTNING STONE SIDEBAR**

This mineral is found throughout Ninyoldah's cave complex. Dwarves call it *lightning stone*. It is highly conductive and exposed deposits attract lightning strikes — giving the substance its name.

**Properties in areas with heavy lightning stone concentrations:**
- All creatures have **disadvantage on saving throws** against lightning damage
- Lightning attacks deal **1 additional damage per damage die** (e.g., *shocking grasp* doing 1d8 becomes 1d8+1; Ninyoldah's breath doing 12d10 becomes 12d10+12)

**Lightning stone value:** 8,000 gp worth is present in Ninyoldah's lair. Mining 1,000 gp of ore takes 1 week and produces 200 pounds of marketable material.

### AREA 1-4B — SHOOTING GALLERY

*A long 20-foot-wide, 30-foot-high passage descends deeper into the mountain. Stalagmites and stalactites are present but have been deliberately broken to clear the passage. The walls, floor, and ceiling are striated with purple-red ore that seems to run everywhere.*

This is the most dangerous approach to the dragon's lair. If the cloakers in Area 1-4 alerted Ninyoldah, she positions herself just around the far bend of this passage and unleashes her breath weapon.

**Lightning Breath Ambush.** Ninyoldah's breath weapon, amplified by the lightning stone, becomes a **60-foot-diameter sphere** at point of impact. Every creature in the sphere makes a **DC 19 Dexterity saving throw** at **disadvantage** (lightning stone property), taking **78 (12d10 + 12) lightning damage** on a failure, half on a success.

After the blast, Ninyoldah calls down in a deep Draconic voice: *"Such to all who trespass across my threshold. Turn back, little serpents, lest I burn you again. This is your only warning."*

**Parley.** A **DC 20 Charisma (Persuasion or Deception) check** convinces Ninyoldah to hold her breath weapon and negotiate. She demands the party remain in this passage during the conversation (see Area 1-4C for what she will say). Violating this condition ends negotiations immediately. Each subsequent refusal of a breath weapon recharge requires another DC 20 check.

### AREA 1-4C — NINYOLDAH'S LAIR

*The walls of this massive cavern are rich with purplish-red minerals and glittering flecks of mica. The floor has been worn smooth by the repeated passage of a heavy scaled body. Low natural shelves divide the space into tiers. One high shelf to the north holds a glittering mound of golden treasure and piled coins. A nearby shelf is littered with broken, leathery egg fragments. Another passage leads east into darkness.*

This is the home of Ninyoldah, an **adult blue dragon** (*Monster Manual*) — but treat her as a **CR 17 creature** (18,000 XP) when defeated in her lair due to the lightning stone amplifying her abilities.

**Ninyoldah's Disposition.** She is hostile to intruders and finds diplomacy with lesser beings beneath her dignity — though she enjoys a good laugh and it has been a while. She knows far more about the raiders than the party does.

If the party has successfully negotiated parley from Area 1-4B, she will speak with them — grudgingly — and reveal the following if the party succeeds on a **DC 20 Charisma (Persuasion or Deception) check**:
- The raiders are a strange mix of humans and humanoids with beast features unlike any natural species
- They carry a "floating altar of metal" she has never seen before, which unleashes fire and death
- She has seen humans and humanoids occasionally traveling between the horde's location and a cave at the base of a nearby mountain
- If the party agrees to depart immediately and not return, she gives directions to both the cave (Area 1-12) and the horde's encampment (Area 1-6)

**Combat.** Ninyoldah will not parley with anyone who has entered her lair proper (Area 1-4C). She uses all available means to destroy intruders, retreating only if reduced to 25 or fewer HP. She cannot collapse tunnels with breath weapon — the lightning stone redirects the energy rather than concentrating it.

**Egg Fragments.** The broken egg shards are from Ninyoldah's last brood, long since whelped and living in their own dens. Interesting curiosity, no value.

**Treasure.** 14,000 gp, 1,800 pp, 15 gems (1,000 gp each), *cloak of elvenkind*, *staff of withering*, *giant slayer battleaxe*, *boots of levitation*. Plus 8,000 gp in extractable lightning stone ore.

### AREA 1-4D — ESCAPE TUNNEL

*A 25-foot-wide, 20-foot-high tunnel winds for nearly half a mile under the mountain before reaching what appears to be a natural rock fall. The barrier contains no lightning stone deposits.*

Ninyoldah's escape route. The dragon can blast through the rock fall with her breath weapon in one round if needed. Otherwise, the obstruction requires a **DC 25 Strength check** and 1 hour of work to clear enough space for a Medium creature to pass.

### AREA 1-5 — THE STONE SPEAKS

*The ground beneath your feet rumbles and tiny stones dance across the exposed rock. A voice like grinding boulders sounds as if the stony ground itself is speaking.*

The party has strayed into the territory of a **galeb duhr** (*Monster Manual*) named **Droll-Durm**. It addresses them in Terran: *"Who walks upon my rocks?"*

If the party cannot communicate (no Terran, no *comprehend languages*): Droll-Durm animates nearby boulders and attacks until the intruders retreat. It does not pursue beyond its territory.

If communication is possible, it is initially **indifferent** (miffed but not aggressive). It is deeply troubled by recent events — the rumblings in the mountain, the "tickling of many feet."

**Shifting Droll-Durm to Friendly.** Telling it that the party has come to investigate the source of the disturbances and restore order: **DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion) check** (from indifferent). If friendly, it shares:

- *"Three moons ago, the stones turned over in their long sleep"* — the earthquake that cleared the ship's upper airlock and caused the computer malfunction that reactivated the pacifier robot
- *"The stones quiver and shiver from time to time"* — the robots clearing the lower cargo hatch and the ship's renewed activity
- *"About two moons ago, a loud rumble and the tickling of many feet"* — the pacifier robot and its troops departing the ship
- Directions to the spaceship: *"The strongest rumblings come from where warmth lingers longest at night and the moon does not sail"* (northwest) — **DC 10 Intelligence check** by a Terran speaker to interpret, otherwise **DC 15**
- Notes on Ninyoldah: *"A shadow that passes across the sun and moon sometimes... leaves sky fire in its wake... dwells behind my head near morning"* (to the northeast)

**Galeb Duhr's Gratitude.** If the party returns after ending the pacifier robot threat, Droll-Durm reveals a small cave high in the mountains containing a **vein of opals**. Mining it takes 3 days and produces **10,000 gp** in gemstones.

### AREA 1-6 — INVADERS' ENCAMPMENT

*A shallow, broad vale extends to the river. On either side of a central stream are 40 low domes made from taut fabric, each about 10 feet in diameter. A hundred or more humanoid creatures move about in an animalistic fashion — dog-people prowling like caged beasts. Among the bestial humanoids are beautiful humans in shimmering garments. On a low rise overlooking all of it: a floating metal platform, 10 feet long, 6 feet wide, bristling with rod-like protrusions. It seems to watch.*

This is the base camp of the invasion force. **Show Handout #61** if the party gets a clear view of the pacifier robot.

**Force Composition:**
- 1 **pacifier robot** (Level VII Appendix B) — on its observation rise
- 75 **type one biological entities** — patrolling, resting in domes, or moving between the camp and forward positions
- 25 **type two biological entities** — guarding the perimeter and on heavy-work details
- 15 **androids** (Level I Appendix B) — command assistants to the pacifier robot, managing the force

**The Domes.** Each dome holds 1d4 foam sleeping pads, a blanket, and occasionally a spare non-technological weapon (battleaxe). Nothing of interest.

**Supply Tents.** 1-in-8 chance any given tent is a supply tent containing: 12 replacement axes, a case of 25 needler clips, and 1d6 spare needler pistols (see Appendix C, Level I).

**Direct Attack.** Suicidal. The DM should allow the players to attempt it but give every opportunity for retreat. The pacifier robot alone could destroy most parties. The biological entities fight to the death. The androids use firearms and grenades. If the party engages even a fraction of the force, the entire camp responds within 2 rounds.

**Development — Infiltration and Surveillance.** The party must make a **DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) group check** every **20 minutes** they remain in or around the camp:
- **First failed check:** 1d6 type one biological entities notice something suspicious and investigate the party's area
- **Second failed check:** 2d6 type one biological entities + 1d4 androids respond; the pacifier robot activates its sensors
- **Third failed check:** Full alert. The pacifier robot engages; all forces converge on the party's position. This is the full force described above

**Indirect Resolution.** The most effective approach is to bypass this camp entirely, reach the ship, and use the monitoring station in Level VII Area 1 to shut down or recall the pacifier robot. If the robot is deactivated here, the camp becomes leaderless within 1 round — androids wait 48 hours for new orders before returning to the ship; biological entities disperse into the wilderness.

### AREA 1-7 — STONE GIANT CLAN

*A mountainside cave complex, its entrance reinforced with a crude palisade of massive logs and boulders. This could only have been built by creatures of tremendous size. Sounds of grieving come from within — low, rhythmic chanting in a deep language you don't recognize.*

The stone giant clan of Patriarch Froddandan occupies this cave complex. The patriarch is dead — killed two days ago by the pacifier robot's laser battery and fragmentation grenades when he and Frothar were out on a "dream walk" (nighttime vision quest) and blundered into the horde's encampment.

**Giants Present:**
- **Frothar** (wounded male, the survivor) — in the main chamber, has the story of Froddandan's death
- **Frekyah** (matriarch) — in the main chamber, focused on grief and preparation for burial
- **Futhli** (Frekyah's daughter) — in the main chamber or females' quarters
- **Fumma** (female) — in Area 1-7C (females' quarters), mending Froddandan's burial clothes
- **Falknur** (male) — in Area 1-7D (males' quarters), sharpening Froddandan's weapons for interment
- **Frikita** (young female) — in Area 1-7B (dining hall), preparing the funeral meal
- **Gurggh** — a cave bear (stats as polar bear) bonded to Fumma
- **Frummach** — may be absent (out on dream walk, potential random encounter; see Area 1-7 random encounter)

**Reception.** If the party arrives alone without Frummach: the giants treat them as suspicious intruders. Initial reaction is **hostile** — they will not attack immediately but demand the party state their purpose and prove they are not connected to the "sky fire altar" that killed their patriarch.

If **Frummach accompanies the party**: reception is **indifferent** — the giants listen, and appropriate roleplay can shift them to **friendly**.

**DM Note.** Stone giants believe the above-ground world is a "dream world" filled with symbolism and omens. Frothar's account of Froddandan's death is told in surreal metaphors — glowing mushroom domes, a floating altar, sky fire. A **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check** recalls this stone giant cultural belief, making Frothar's account understandable rather than confusing.

**Frothar's Account (if reached with Frummach or after winning trust):**
- They were on a dream walk when drawn by a "brilliant glow in a shallow valley"
- They saw "glowing domes like mushroom caps" and "tiny figures" (the invaders' camp)
- "An altar of shining metal" floated among them (the pacifier robot)
- "Dancing motes of fire appeared around them" (laser targeting sights)
- The "sky fire" erupted from the altar; Froddandan fell; Frothar carried his body home
- This occurred two nights ago; Frothar can give rough directions to the valley (Area 1-6) — **DC 15 Wisdom (Survival)** to correctly interpret his stone-giant directions

**The Clan's Decision.** Frekyah and Futhli have decided: they will take Froddandan deep underground to a secret stone giant burial sepulcher and never return to the surface. The entire clan agrees. They will not help the party in their expedition — they want no more connection to the above-ground world that killed their leader.

**Treasure (Frothar):**
- Carved ivory amulet (250 gp)
- Two beaten copper bracelets (100 gp each)
- Pouch: 20 gems (100 gp each)

**Treasure (Frekyah and Futhli, combined):**
- Ivory earrings (100 gp per set × 2)
- Pouches: 2d10 gems each (50 gp each)

### AREA 1-7B — DINING HALL

*A smaller cavern with an open hearth and a table made from a granite slab surrounded by stalagmite "tree stump" seats. A young female stone giant works at the hearth preparing food.*

**Frikita** (young female stone giant) is preparing the funeral meal. She flees to Area 1-7D if intruders enter. She will not fight. If caught and communicated with gently, she can say only that she is "making food for the goodbye."

### AREA 1-7C — FEMALES' QUARTERS

*Four large beds made from mounds of bear hides. Narrow doors between the beds. A large basin of water in one corner. A cave bear crouches near a female stone giant working over a set of ornate clothing.*

**Fumma** (female stone giant) is mending Froddandan's burial clothes. Her cave bear **Gurggh** fights to protect her. Fumma will flee immediately if given the chance, while Gurggh fights to the death.

**Treasure:**
- Fumma: mammoth ivory bracelets (200 gp each × 2), necklace of uncut quartz (250 gp)
- Among personal belongings: three more quartz necklaces (200 gp, 300 gp, 500 gp), silver bracelet (300 gp), pouch with 150 pp
- Froddandan's burial clothes: rare furs (1,000 gp value)
- Stone idols: no intrinsic value; 50 gp each to collectors

### AREA 1-7D — MALES' QUARTERS

*Five large beds of bear hides around an open hearth. Giant-sized spears, clubs, and clothing. One male stone giant deliberately sharpens his patriarch's weapons.*

**Falknur** (male stone giant) is sharpening Froddandan's weapons for burial. He fights if threatened but prefers the party leave.

**Treasure (Falknur):**

- Giant-sized silver torc (500 gp)
- Six more gems (100 gp each) among belongings
- Polished obsidian statuette of a bald humanoid head, 3 feet tall, 200 pounds (1,000 gp)

### AREA 1-7E — UNDERDARK EXIT

*A great pile of boulders blocks the tunnel at the far end of the complex. These boulders are enormous — moving them would require giants working together.*

At least **three stone giants working in concert** can clear this passage. Beyond it lies a miles-long tunnel winding into the Underdark — the clan's intended route to inter Froddandan. The DM decides what (if anything) lies deeper in the Underdark beyond this point.

### AREA 1-8 — BASILISKS' DEN

*A dark cave mouth, halfway up the mountain slope. A narrow trail winds up to it. Something strange half-buried in the scree near the entrance — a strange statue of a mountain goat, partially consumed.*

A clutch of **4 basilisks** (*Monster Manual*) inhabit this cave (60-foot diameter, 10-foot-wide, 8-foot-high entrance).

**Discovery.** A **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check** while climbing the path notices the statue near the entrance. A **DC 20 Intelligence (Nature) check** identifies it as a petrified mountain goat. The basilisks fight fearlessly to defend their home and are **immune to the frightened condition** inside it.

**Treasure.** Among petrified mountain animal remains, there is a half-devoured petrified ogre. Around one stony finger: a **ring of x-ray vision**.

### AREA 1-9 — ANDROID BIVOUAC

*Two shining silver domes rest among the rocks — 3 feet high, 10 feet in diameter, made from taut cloth-like material. A seam in each suggests a small hemispherical door. Six attractive humans in strange shimmering clothing perform activities with small boxes and rods.*

**Six androids** (see Level I Appendix B) are taking atmospheric sensor readings and feeding data to the spaceship's central computer. Two **all-weather shelters** (see Level VII Appendix C) are deployed. The androids attack if noticed and will not negotiate.

**Combat Gear:**
- Three androids: needler pistols (Level I Appendix C)
- Two androids: 1 fragmentation grenade each (Level I Appendix C)
- One android: 1 sleep gas grenade (Level I Appendix C)

**Treasure:** Two **atmosphere analyzers** (Level IV Appendix C) and six **needler clips** (Level I Appendix C).

### AREA 1-10 — BLASTED BEAR

*The corpse of a large four-legged creature lies on its side among the stones. It appears to have been 12 feet long and covered in fur — but the body is now blackened and cracked as if subjected to an immense flash of heat that cooked it where it stood.*

A cave bear killed by the pacifier robot's blaster rifle. **DC 12 Wisdom (Nature) check:** Identifies it as a cave bear, a formidable beast weighing over a ton. **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check:** Determined by a single stroke, a blast of intense heat; killed at least two months ago, yet unmolested by scavengers (the burnt flesh apparently repels predators).

**Tracks. DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check:** Reveals three sets of trails radiating from this location (different ages):
- Oldest: leads north up the valley (toward Area 1-11 and then Area 1-7 stone giants)
- Somewhat older: heads southwest along the riverbank (toward the sacked strongholds)
- Newest: heads northeast before becoming lost in the rocks (toward the current encampment Area 1-6)

These mark the pacifier robot's initial deployment route from the ship, its first raids, and its current patrol area.

### AREA 1-11 — CAVE TRAIL

*A stretch of rocky soil is visible among the boulders and steep mountain slopes at this point. Tracks — some booted, some barefoot and clawed — are clearly visible. Many are older and worn, headed south. A few more recent ones head northward up the valley.*

**DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check:** Easy to follow this trail northward to Area 1-12. Even on a failure, the high valley walls limit possible routes — following the valley north eventually reveals the cave entrance close enough to spot.

### AREA 1-12 — THE MYSTERIOUS CAVE

*Set into a high, rocky outcrop near the head of the valley is something clearly not natural: a massive door of dark gray metal, perhaps 30 feet wide and 40 feet tall. Strange shapes and markings are pressed into the metal surface. The door stands open — just barely.*

This is the main entrance to the spaceship. Refer to the **Chapter 3 introductory text** (the "Start" section of the original adventure) for the full description of the approach, the door mechanism, the entry tunnel, and the party's first moments inside Level I.

The cave entrance reveals nothing of what lies beyond until the party commits to entering. The metal door is open just enough to allow entry (the ship's cargo ramp/entry system was damaged in the crash and hasn't properly closed in years). Beyond is the beginning of Level I.

## APPENDIX: CONNECTING THE CHAPTERS

**The full adventure sequence** using this chapter:
1. Party receives mission from relevant faction or Baron Urhar (Area 1-1)
2. Cross the Tumbledown River (Area 1-2)
3. Investigate the sacked stronghold for evidence (Area 1-3) — optional
4. Wilderness exploration leads to various encounters (random table + Areas 1-5 through 1-11)
5. Enter the ship through Area 1-12
6. Work through Levels I–VII
7. Resolve the pacifier robot via monitoring station (Level VII Area 1) or direct combat
8. Return to Baron Urhar (or faction leaders) with evidence of the ship's nature and the threat's resolution

**The pacifier robot loop:** If the party stopped the active pacifier robot via the monitoring station, the horde at Area 1-6 falls apart within 48 hours. Baron Urhar can witness this happening — the attacks stop, scouts report the horde dispersing. The adventure concludes without necessarily fighting the robot directly.

**Ninyoldah's long game:** The dragon now knows a strange cave exists with creatures in metallic clothing and floating altars. She has no interest in pursuing this further — unless the party takes something from her hoard or kills her in a way that draws attention from her surviving brood. The DM may want to track whether Ninyoldah becomes a future antagonist.
