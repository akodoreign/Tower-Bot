Section I · Overview

The contract, in brief.

Glass Sigil has spent six months tracing a rumor down through the Cobbleway merchant quarter — a rumor that hardens, the deeper one digs, into something resembling a map. Beneath the old counting houses sleeps a vault, sealed in the last days of the Prior Compact and never reopened. What lies within is not listed in any ledger they will share.

The contract is quiet. The Sigil needs a discreet crew to bypass the vault's wardens, retrieve what sleeps inside, and return before the rival Obsidian Lotus — who have also begun sniffing at the masonry — catch the scent. Success buys the crew a standing favor with the Sigil and a purse that does not need to be explained. Failure is unlikely to leave witnesses.

Player Briefing Grandmaster Vex will meet the party in the upper solar of Glass Sigil's counting house at dusk. She provides: partial floorplans, the name of a reliable fence, and a hard deadline of three nights before the Lotus move.

The party has three nights. The Sigil will pay for extraction, but not for rescue. If the job goes loud, there is no cavalry and the vault's guardians will not negotiate. The Cobbleway watch has been paid to look away — but only until dawn of the third night. After that, every badge in the quarter has orders to arrest anyone found near the door.

DM guidance · theft This is a heist. Give the party at least three viable approaches: social (seduce a ledger clerk), stealth (the servant's stair), direct (blow the east wall). Reward their plan. Success is getting the item and escaping — or paying the cost of staying too long.

The item itself remains undescribed in Vex's briefing. She calls it only "the sleeper." Investigation leads in Section III will let clever parties learn more before committing — and discover that Glass Sigil has not been entirely honest about why they want it back.

A rival crew from Obsidian Lotus is active in the quarter. They may reach the door first. They may try to hire the party off. They may simply wait, and then rob the party on the way out. All three should be on the table.

Section II · Five Acts

Planning, infiltration, and the Vault.

Act I
The Solar Meeting
≈ 25 min · roleplay

Grandmaster Vex receives the party at dusk in the upper solar of Glass Sigil's counting house. The fire is lit but low. Three ledgers are open on the table; a fourth, in a glass box, is closed. She is direct. She wants the sleeper recovered. She will answer two questions honestly and one question evasively; the party must choose which to ask.

She provides: partial floorplans of the Cobbleway vault (Handout A), a writ of passage through the merchant quarter, and the name Orrin Keel — a fence who owes the Sigil a favor and keeps a stall at the night market.

Skill · Insight DC 14 Vex is lying about the age of the vault. It is younger than she claims, and she knows whose seal is on the door.
Act II
Scouting the Cobbleway
≈ 40 min · mixed

The party has one night of reconnaissance before the watch rotation reliably changes. The Cobbleway district is a knot of counting houses, a covered market, a temple to the Clerk God, and the old grain cellars under which the vault sits.

Three investigation leads (see §III) become available. The party may pursue all three if they split up or move quickly; otherwise, two is realistic. Each lead reshapes the final heist in a concrete way.

Complication · Lotus eyes A Lotus scout tails the party after their second lead. Perception DC 16 to notice. If caught and questioned, she offers the party 1,500 EC to walk away. If killed, the Lotus will retaliate in Act V.
Act III
Through the Vault Door
≈ 45 min · skill + combat

The party enters the vault on the third night. Their chosen approach — social, stealth, or direct — determines the shape of this act but not its beats: first the outer corridor (traps and warding), then the antechamber (the vault guardians stir), then the door itself.

The door reads the party's intent. Anyone whose name appears in Glass Sigil's ledger of oaths passes untouched. Everyone else takes 2d10 radiant damage, Wisdom save DC 15 for half, on first contact. Clever parties may find the inscription (Investigation DC 18) and bluff past.

The guardians — three Iron Golems bound to the vault — wake as the door opens. See Encounter 1 on page 6. They will not pursue beyond the antechamber.

Section II · continued

The sleeper, and the long night after.

Act IV
The Inner Sanctum
≈ 25 min · roleplay

Past the vault door, a single round chamber. In its center, on a plinth of worn marble, rests a child's wooden figure — a soldier, one arm missing, its paint nearly gone. This is the sleeper. A held breath hangs in the air. Any character who touches the figure hears, faintly, a woman's voice ask: "Are you kind?"

DM · the twist The sleeper is bound to the spirit of Vex's sister, lost in the Compact Wars and not dead — only stored. Vex wants the figure destroyed, not recovered. The briefing is a lie of omission. The party learns this only if they ask the figure directly (it answers honestly) or if they succeed at the Act I Insight check and follow the thread.

The choice belongs to the party. Return the figure to Vex, and the sister is consigned to silence. Destroy it, and the sister passes on — but the Sigil's payment is forfeit. Keep it, and the party now carries a relic that will find its own way into later sessions.

Act V
The Long Walk Out
≈ 30 min · combat or social

The party leaves the vault before dawn. Outside, the Lotus are waiting — unless the party bought them off in Act II, killed their scout, or bypassed the quarter entirely. What follows is either a running battle through the grain cellars (see Encounter 2 · Cobbleway Ambush) or a tense standoff resolved by the Deception or Persuasion of a Lotus offer (DC 17 · contested).

    Keel will sell the party the vault's interior layout for 200 EC or a story worth telling. He knows the antechamber is trapped but not the nature of the guardians.
    WHY go here Reveals the guardians' save DC and the existence of the inscription on the door. Makes Act III survivable.
  • Lead · The Clerk God's Priest Brother Sennis · Temple of the Clerk God, morning mass
    Sennis keeps the Cobbleway's older records. For a donation (100 EC) he will produce a ledger listing the vault's original sealing oath — and the seven names on it. One of the names is Vex's sister.
    WHY go here Unlocks the Act IV twist. Without this lead, the figure's question lands cold. With it, the party has context.
  • Lead · The Retired Warden Ferrun Ashe · former vault warden · Ember & Ash boarding house
    Ashe drinks heavily, speaks reluctantly, and will only open up after Persuasion DC 15 or a shared drink. He can describe the guardians' weakness — they freeze for one round when the vault's own sigil is shown to them.
    WHY go here Gives combat a non-damage-race angle. Reduces Encounter 1 difficulty from Deadly to Hard.
